I am extremely happy to share that I have been awarded a Starting Grant by the European Research Council for my project **UREikA — UltraRelativistic Eikonal Amplitudes**.

[ERC-StG 2026 announcement](https://erc.europa.eu/news-events/news/erc-2026-starting-grants-results)

This grant will allow me to build my research group to investigate some of the most fundamental ideas underlying General Relativity's description of gravity, and address long-standing questions about its behaviour at high energies.

Stay tuned for more information and for upcoming job opportunities!

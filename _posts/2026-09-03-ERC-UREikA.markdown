I am extremely happy to share that I have been awarded a Starting Grant by the European Research Council for my project **UREikA — UltraRelativistic Eikonal Amplitudes**.

[ERC-StG 2026 announcement](https://erc.europa.eu/news-events/news/erc-2026-starting-grants-results)

This grant will allow me to build my research group to investigate some of the most fundamental ideas underlying General Relativity's description of gravity, and address long-standing questions about its behaviour at high energies. More information can be found on the [IPhT](https://www.ipht.fr/en/2026/09/carlo-heissenberg-ipht-cea-cnrs-has-been-awarded-an-erc-starting-grant-for-the-ureika-project-ultrarelativistic-eikonal-amplitudes-gravitational-scattering-at-high-energies/), [CEA-DRF](https://www.cea.fr/drf/Pages/Actualites/Vie-de-la-DRF/2026/Quatre-chercheurs-drf-laureats-erc-starting-grant.aspx), and [CNRS-INP](https://www.inp.cnrs.fr/fr/personne/carlo-heissenberg) websites.

Stay tuned for more information and for upcoming job opportunities!

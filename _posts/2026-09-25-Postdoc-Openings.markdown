I am recruiting two postdoctoral researchers at IPhT, CEA Saclay, as part of my ERC-StG project **UREikA** — *UltraRelativistic Eikonal Amplitudes*:

- A **three-year position** on scattering amplitudes and gravitational-wave observables, in particular emitted energy, deflection angle and gravitational waveforms. The focus is on their high-energy limit, eikonal exponentiation, soft/collinear and self-force resummations.

- A **two-year position** (which may be renewed for one more year) on holographic approaches for gravitational waves, including Celestial/Carroll holography, and their connections with soft theorems and asymptotic symmetries.

Appointments are expected to begin on October 1st, 2027; the exact starting date may be negotiable. Applicants are expected to hold a PhD in theoretical physics by the starting date.

The full job listing, along with other related openings at IPhT, is available on Academic Jobs Online [here](https://academicjobsonline.org/ajo/jobs/32841).

Applications should include a CV, publication list, a brief research statement, and 2 to 3 reference letters. The deadline for submission is fixed for **December 18th, 2026**.
